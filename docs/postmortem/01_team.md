## Advice to our next teams
1. I believe it is important not to waste time redoing work, so to avoid that you need to make sure it is high quality by ensuring it passes static analysis + tests when it goes in on the 1st attempt.  Also identify and build out the core features of the app before branching out into extraneous stuff, these things always tend to take longer than expected so you dont waste time on extras before getting the main stuff done.
